# Results

## Peak-Based Methods

Peak-based methods such as ChIA-PET and HiChIP rely on a ChIP enrichment step to identify protein binding sites as high-confidence peak loci across the genome. This approach has been demonstrated to robustly capture genome-wide chromatin interactions mediated by specific transcription factors (TFs), including ERα (estrogen receptor alpha), RNAPII (RNA polymerase II), and CTCF (CCCTC-binding factor); components of the cohesin complex; and histone proteins.

## scHi-C Clustering Benchmark

To evaluate single-cell clustering performance, Chr3D was benchmarked against **Higashi** on a five-cluster dataset derived from Higashi's published reference labels. Chr3D generates a bin-based feature matrix using a single fragment endpoint per read, and three positional encodings were systematically compared:

- **pos1** — left endpoint of fragment
- **pos2** — right endpoint of fragment
- **both** — concatenation of both endpoints

Clustering using `pos1` or `pos2` alone yielded an ARI on par with Higashi, outperforming the `both` variant. The degradation in performance when incorporating both endpoints suggests that including redundant or noisy positional information from the second fragment end introduces signal dilution rather than complementary context — consistent with the sparse and asymmetric nature of single-cell contact data.
